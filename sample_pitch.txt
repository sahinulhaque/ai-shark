TiffinAI — home-cooked meals, personalised by AI.

The problem: 40 million urban Indians eat lunch out every workday, and most of it is
oily, repetitive, and unhealthy. Home-style tiffin services exist, but they send the same
fixed menu to everyone and have terrible retention.

Our solution: TiffinAI is a daily tiffin subscription where an AI plans each customer's
meals around their health goals, allergies, and taste feedback, then routes orders to a
network of vetted home chefs in their neighbourhood. Every meal is home-cooked, tuned to
you, and cheaper than eating out.

Traction: 1,200 active subscribers across Pune and Bengaluru, growing 22% month on month.
Average revenue per user is Rs 4,500/month at a 28% gross margin. Customer acquisition cost
is Rs 900 and we're seeing 6-month retention of 55%. We have 180 home chefs onboarded.

The ask: we're raising Rs 4 crore for 8% to expand to four more cities, build the chef app,
and improve the recommendation engine. The Indian food-tech market is worth 50 billion dollars
and growing 25% a year.
