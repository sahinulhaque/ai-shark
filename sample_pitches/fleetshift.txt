FleetShift — AI dispatch for India's trucking fleets.

Problem: 3.5 million mid-size trucking operators in India still assign loads over
WhatsApp and phone calls. Trucks run 35% empty on return trips, bleeding money.

Solution: FleetShift is a dispatch platform where an AI matches available trucks to
return loads in real time, cutting empty miles and paperwork. Operators pay per truck
per month.

Traction: 900 trucks live across Nagpur, Surat and Ludhiana. Revenue Rs 62 lakh/month,
growing 18% MoM at a 71% gross margin. Empty-return miles down from 35% to 19% for our
customers. Net revenue retention is 130%. CAC Rs 6,000, payback in under 3 months.

Ask: raising Rs 6 crore for 9% to expand to 8 corridors and build the load-matching engine.
